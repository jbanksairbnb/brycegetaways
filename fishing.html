Fishing the Valley

There's a 44-acre lake five minutes from the driveway, one of Virginia's best stocked trout streams over the next ridge, and a smallmouth river winding through seven bends of farmland below. Whether you've brought a fly rod, a spinning outfit, or a bucket and a kid with a bobber, there's water here worth a morning. Here's where to go, what's in it, and what you'll need first.

Show
01

Lake Laura

~5 min drive
  • Easy access
  • 44 acres
  • 30 ft deep
  • Bass · panfish · catfish

The closest water, and the easiest morning of the lot. Bryce Resort's lake impounds the headwaters of Big Stony Creek, and Virginia's biologists list largemouth bass, bluegill, pumpkinseed, crappie, yellow perch, channel catfish and even muskellunge in it. There's shoreline access across the breast of the dam and around the boat launch, plus a primitive launch at the upper end for a canoe or a kayak from the resort. Bass run to a five-fish daily limit with only one over 15 inches; sunfish and crappie have no minimum size. Fishing is open from an hour before sunrise to an hour after sunset.

Access note: the lake is resort-owned, so confirm current access, day passes and boat rules with Bryce Resort before you head down — the rules there are theirs, not the state's.

02

Big Stony Creek

~20 min drive
  • Wade
  • Stocked trout
  • Along Rt 675
  • Oct–June

Harry Murray, who has run the fly shop down in Edinburg since 1962, calls the Big Stoney one of the finest stocked trout streams in Virginia — and it's the same creek that feeds Lake Laura, coming at you from the other side of the ridge. It runs along Route 675 west of Edinburg with plenty of pull-offs and public water, holding stocked rainbows through the cool months and rock bass and smallmouth in the warmer stretches lower down. Bring waders in spring; a short rod earns its keep in the tighter, brushier pools.

03

North Fork Shenandoah — the Seven Bends

~30 min drive
  • Wade or float
  • Smallmouth
  • Riffle & ledge
  • Summer

The valley's smallmouth river, and the quieter of the two forks — the South Fork gets the crowds on the far side of Massanutten Mountain. The middle stretch between Woodstock and Edinburg holds both numbers and size, and the bedrock ledges running down toward Strasburg are the classic structure to work. It fishes well from a kayak or on foot in the riffles.

Where to get in: Seven Bends State Park has a concrete ramp at the end of Lupton Road; Chapman Landing Road sits just downstream of Edinburg, Red Banks Road between Edinburg and Mount Jackson, and Hollingsworth Road east of Woodstock — all popular with waders and small boats.

04

Passage Creek at Elizabeth Furnace

~45 min drive
  • Wade
  • Stocked trout
  • Delayed Harvest
  • Fort Valley

Worth the drive for the setting alone. Passage Creek runs 38 miles down the length of Fort Valley — the hidden valley inside Massanutten Mountain — before it meets the North Fork, and Virginia stocks it from the hatchery at its northern end. Expect rainbows, with browns and brook trout when the hatchery has them. Part of the creek is put-and-take; another stretch is managed as Delayed Harvest, which extends the fishing from October into late May but asks you to release what you catch. Access is easy along Fort Valley Road through the Elizabeth Furnace recreation area. By high summer the trout are gone or dormant, but sunfish, rock bass, fallfish and chub keep a light rod busy.

Before You Cast

Licenses, seasons & the small print

Get the license first

Virginia sells fishing licenses online through Go Outdoors Virginia, the state's official portal — buy it on your phone in the car if you forgot. Sixteen and over needs one.

Add the trout license

A separate trout license is required on designated stocked waters from October 1 through June 15 — that covers Big Stony and Passage Creek in their best months. As of the 2026 fees it costs the same for residents and visitors.

What it costs

Roughly $23 for a resident freshwater license and $47 for a non-resident, with the trout stamp about $23 on top. Check current pricing at purchase — the state adjusts fees.

Trout run cool

Stocking and the trout fishing that follows run from fall through spring. By July the valley streams are warm and the trout are done; that's when the smallmouth on the North Fork come into their own.

Lake Laura is private

State creel limits apply, but access is Bryce Resort's to grant. Ask at the resort about current rules, day access and whether you can put a boat on before you load the car.

Clean your gear

Dry your waders and boots between watersheds. Didymo and other hitchhikers move on felt soles, and these small mountain streams don't recover quickly.

Guides & Gear

Someone who knows the water

Murray's Fly Shop
~20 min
Edinburg, VA

The institution. Harry Murray opened the shop in the middle of Edinburg in 1962 and it's been one of the East Coast's best-stocked fly shops ever since — flies tied for these exact streams, plus instruction and guided walk-and-wade days for trout and smallmouth. Call 540-984-4212 to book.
