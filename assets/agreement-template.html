The Owner does not collect a separate upfront security deposit. The Tenant agrees to reimburse the Owner for any damage to the property or its furnishings beyond normal wear and tear, based on proof of repair costs and appropriate quotes provided by the Owner.

Up to two (2) dogs are permitted, provided the pet fee is paid and the dog(s) are disclosed at the time of booking. Additional dogs or other animals require the Owner’s prior written approval. 9. Termination

The Owner has the right to inspect the premises with prior notice as provided by applicable State law. Should the Tenant violate any term of this Agreement, the rental period shall terminate immediately in accordance with State law. The Tenant waives all rights to process if they fail to vacate upon termination of the rental period, and shall vacate the premises at the expiration date and time of this Agreement.

10. Maintenance & Repairs

The Tenant shall maintain the premises in good, clean, ready-to-rent condition and use the premises only in a careful and lawful manner. The Tenant shall leave the premises ready to rent — defined by the Owner as immediately habitable by the next guests — at the end of the rental period, and shall pay for maintenance and repairs if the premises are left in a lesser condition or damaged beyond normal wear and tear, based on proof of repair costs and quotes. If repairs are required during the stay, the Tenant shall contact the Owner immediately. Pest-control service is available; the Tenant will contact the Owner if it is needed.

11. Trash

All waste generated during the rental period must be kept in tied plastic trash bags and placed in the waste bins (on the street or in the house) — never directly on the ground — to keep animals and insects out.

12. Subletting

The Tenant shall not sublet the property.

13. Quiet Enjoyment

The Tenant shall behave in a civilized manner, be a good neighbor, and respect the rights of surrounding property owners. The Tenant shall not create noise or disturbances likely to disturb or annoy neighbors. Quiet hours begin at 11:00 PM, when outdoor noise should be kept to a minimum. Creating a disturbance is grounds for immediate termination of this Agreement, after which the Tenant shall immediately vacate.

14. HOA / Sky Bryce Association Rules

The Tenant must adhere to the rules and guidelines of the Sky Bryce Association, Inc. (SBA). Repeated violations or disregard for HOA rules are grounds for immediate termination of this Agreement. General Conduct Policy:

  1. Observe quiet hours between 11:00 PM and 7:00 AM.
  2. The posted speed limit on the private roads in Sky Bryce Subdivision is 25 mph.
  3. Hunting or discharge of firearms is not allowed in Sky Bryce Subdivision.
  4. Fireworks are not allowed.
  5. No wood harvesting from property you do not own.

The Board of Directors will investigate complaints received from the SBA, Sky Bryce Patrol, or the Shenandoah County Sheriff’s Office and take appropriate action.
