Shenandoah Wine Country

The valley around Bryce is quietly one of Virginia's best-kept wine and beer regions — the oldest winery in the state, award-winning small producers, farm breweries, and orchard cideries, most of them a short, scenic drive from Aspen Way South. Here are our favorites, closest first.

Vineyards & Wineries

Tasting rooms in the valley

Cave Ridge Vineyard
~20 min
Mount Jackson, VA

Estate wines and mountain views at the foot of Great North Mountain — known for its Riesling and the Fossil Hill reds.

Map & hours ↗
Third Hill at DeMello Vineyards
~20 min
Quicksburg, VA

A family-run tasting room beneath historic Third Hill, with sweeping valley views and small-batch Virginia wines.

Map & hours ↗
Shenandoah Vineyards
~20 min
Edinburg, VA

Virginia's oldest winery, founded in 1976 — a relaxed patio, long valley views, and decades-honed Shenandoah reds and whites.

Map & hours ↗
Wolf Gap Vineyard & Winery
~25 min
Edinburg, VA

A quiet, dog-friendly vineyard tucked toward Wolf Gap, with estate-grown wines and a laid-back tasting barn.

Map & hours ↗
Muse Vineyards
~25 min
Woodstock, VA

Award-winning wines — its flagship Clio has topped Virginia's best — on a striking estate along the Shenandoah River.

Map & hours ↗
North Mountain Vineyard & Winery
~30 min
Maurertown, VA

A hilltop winery and bistro with a European feel — wood-fired fare and long views out over the valley.

Breweries & Cideries

Farm brews & orchard cider

Pale Fire Brewing — Basye Taproom
~5 min
Basye, VA

The closest pour to your door — rotating Pale Fire drafts and Detroit-style pizza, right in the heart of Basye.

Map & hours ↗
Swover Creek Farms & Brewery
~20 min
Edinburg, VA

A working century farm and brewery — farm-brewed beer, wood-fired pizza, a dog park, and pick-your-own in season.

Map & hours ↗
Woodstock Brewhouse
~25 min
Woodstock, VA

Craft beer in a beautifully restored industrial building, with big outdoor seating in downtown Woodstock.

Old Hill Cider at Showalter's Orchard
