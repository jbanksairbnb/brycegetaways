Before You Book

Good to know

Bringing a Dog

Up to two well-behaved dogs are welcome. Please keep pets off couches and cushions; a one-time $50 pet cleaning fee applies and pet-related damage requires reimbursement. Let us know in advance and we'll add a cleaner to make the home hair-free.

Nature Comes With the Mountains

As part of the mountain experience you may see the occasional insect, especially from August to November. We use regular, pet-safe pest control and perimeter traps — but nature sometimes wins.

Sun & Climate

Our large windows offer stunning views and can bring extra heat in the afternoons. The home has three central AC units to keep things cool and comfortable when used appropriately.

Super-Fast Wi-Fi

High-speed Shentel mesh Wi-Fi is perfect for video calls, streaming, and remote work. We've hosted up to eight guests on conference calls at once with no issues.

Layout & Sound

The open layout carries sound between levels, so it can be lively — earplugs are provided for lighter sleepers.

Parking & Access

Ample parking in front on a private, low-traffic street; roads are well maintained in winter. Self check-in with a lockbox, and the whole home and both decks are yours.
