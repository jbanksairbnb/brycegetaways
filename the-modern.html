The Modern

Luxury meets the mountains

Welcome to Bryce Mountain, where contemporary design meets the beauty of the Shenandoah. From the moment you arrive, you'll notice this isn't a typical mountain cabin — floor-to-ceiling windows frame incredible mountain views while warm wood finishes, soaring ceilings, and modern architecture create a space that's both luxurious and inviting.

Whether you're gathering around the oversized kitchen island, relaxing in the hot tub under the stars, enjoying coffee on the deck, or ending the evening around the fire pit, every space was designed to bring people together. The home sits inside Bryce Resort — a two-minute walk to the ski run and mountain bike park, with the ski lodge, the PGA championship golf course, and Lake Laura all about a five-minute drive away.

Before You Book

Good to know

Bringing a Dog

Up to two well-behaved dogs are welcome. A one-time $50 pet cleaning fee helps us maintain the home; please keep pets off furniture and cushions, and any damage beyond normal cleaning may require reimbursement.

You're in the Mountains

Being surrounded by nature means the occasional insect, especially in late summer and fall. We maintain professional pest control and exterior treatments — but nature is part of the mountain experience.

Stay Connected

High-speed Shentel fiber Wi-Fi with a whole-home mesh system is ideal for streaming, video calls, and remote work. We've hosted multiple guests working simultaneously without issue.

Comfort & Climate

Large windows bring beautiful light and some afternoon heat; three separate central HVAC systems keep every level comfortable throughout the year.

Guest Access

The entire home and property are yours — every room, the outdoor living areas, hot tub, fire pit, EV charger, and decks. Self check-in makes arrival simple.

Parking

Easy parking for up to five vehicles between the private driveway and additional street parking.

Your Hosts

Hosted by Jonathan & Anna

We're Airbnb Superhosts, and we host hands-on. Every home is stocked the way we'd want it when we travel ourselves, self check-in makes arrival simple, and we're only a message away throughout your stay — usually replying within the hour.
