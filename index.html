Good to Know

Frequently asked questions

Where are the homes, and how close are the ski slopes?
Both homes sit side by side on Aspen Way South at Bryce Resort in Basye, Virginia. It's about a five-minute walk to the ski slopes, and a short drive to the lodge, the PGA-championship golf course, and Lake Laura.
Can I rent both homes at the same time?
Yes — The Chalet and The Modern are next door to each other, and each sleeps up to 10, so you can host up to 20 guests across both. It's a favorite for extended families and group getaways. Use Check Availability or send us a message to book both together.
Are the homes pet-friendly?
They are — we happily welcome dogs. Just let us know you're bringing a pet when you book so we can have everything ready for them.
How many people does each home sleep?
Each home comfortably sleeps up to 10 guests — 20 in total if you book both. Every bedroom is set up with hotel-quality linens and supremely comfortable beds.
What amenities do the homes have?
Expect a hot tub, fire pit, and expansive decks with sweeping Shenandoah Valley views, plus fully stocked chef's kitchens, fast Wi-Fi, and smart TVs. The Chalet adds a lower-level game room with a pool table, and The Modern has an EV charger.
Do you book direct, or only through Airbnb?
Both — but booking direct with us is the best value, with no extra platform fees. We're Airbnb Superhosts with a 4.99 rating across 275 stays, so you get the same trusted hosting either way. Use Check Availability or message us to book direct.
